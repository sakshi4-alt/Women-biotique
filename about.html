About BookNest

Books make life more interesting.

BookNest is a small online bookstore project created for people who love reading and discovering new ideas.

From inspiring stories to helpful educational books, our goal is to make exploring books simple and enjoyable.

This website is a student project built using HTML, CSS, Bootstrap and JavaScript.
